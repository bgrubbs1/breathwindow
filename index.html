What to do differently

BreathWindow multiplies pollutant concentration by an activity-specific breathing rate and duration. Ozone and heat are added as separate stress components, then the total is adjusted for health sensitivity.

This is a planning aid, not a medical device. Follow local alerts and clinical advice.

Built for real-world use

One forecast is not a plan.

Air quality changes hour by hour. A 30-minute run at 5 PM can deliver far more PM2.5 and ozone than the same run at 7 AM. BreathWindow makes that tradeoff visible before you leave.

01
